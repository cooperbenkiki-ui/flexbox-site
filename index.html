Found Type

Looking for different typography across campus was an interesting experience, as I realized how it isn't something that I would usually think about. Most type around campus is very simple, as its focused on being as easily readable as possible, but there was a lot more variation when looking at posters. My focus when looking around ended up being on trying to find variations in layout and function, such as the campus map vs. the stairway indicator.
